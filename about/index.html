Race engineering

How a site gets from a blank file to the grid.

  1. 01

    Brief first

    Who the page is for, the one thing they should do, and the three reasons they might not. That goes on paper before any layout.

  2. 02

    Many directions

    Several looks for the same brief, then I keep the one that fits the client. The café set is 23 of these.

  3. 03

    Measure the slop

    My own anti-slop playbook, plus the Impeccable detector, flags AI-template habits: gradient text, glow shadows, filler copy. Target is zero findings.

  4. 04

    Test like race day

    Playwright runs at desktop and phone width, in dark mode and with reduced motion, and checks for console errors, broken images and sideways scroll.

How we brew

Brewed through cloth, served in thick porcelain.

Our kopi starts with robusta beans roasted with butter and sugar. That roast gives kopitiam coffee its dark colour and smoky, caramel smell.

We steep the grounds and strain them through a cloth sock filter, pouring back and forth to get a smooth, full body.

Every cup is served straight away in a thick ceramic cup that keeps it hot.

Race engineering

RADIO #26Same checklist before every race.

  1. 01

    Brief first

    Who it's for, the one thing they should do, and why they might not. Written down before any layout.

  2. 02

    Try several setups

    A few looks for one brief, then keep the one that fits. The café lab is that habit, 22 times.

  3. 03

    Measure the slop

    The Impeccable detector and my own checklist flag template habits like gradient text and glow shadows. Target: zero.

  4. 04

    Race-day checks

    Playwright at desktop and phone width, dark mode and reduced motion. A console error, broken image or sideways scroll is a DNF.

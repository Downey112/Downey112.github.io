Eighteen seats around one timber counter. Phones on silent. Laptops away after 11:00 on weekends.

Hening has 18 seats around a shared timber counter. The room stays free of background music and group chatter, so you can read, write, or drink your coffee without distraction.

Phones stay on silent at all times. On weekends we keep a quiet-room rule, with no laptops after 11:00. If you need to take a call, step outside to Jalan Telawi.

How we brew

  1. We roast small batches each week for our house blend and single origin.
  2. We weigh and grind every dose to order at the counter.
  3. We hand-drip each filter cup slowly with fresh hot water.

Questions

Why are filter coffees RM 18 to RM 22?

We roast single-origin beans in-house in small batches. For a simpler morning cup, espresso starts at RM 10 and kaya toast is RM 10.

Will it be too crowded or noisy to read?

No. There are only 18 seats, phones stay on silent, and laptops are put away after 11:00 on weekends. You can reserve your seat ahead of time.

Do I need to know specialty coffee to order?

No. You do not need tasting notes or brewing ratios. Ask about the beans if you are curious, or order a White or a Kopi Hening.

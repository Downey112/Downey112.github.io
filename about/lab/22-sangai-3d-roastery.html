Hotspot 01

The roaster drum

Green beans tumble in a hot steel drum: dry heat, no butter, no sugar, no oil. That is sangai. It brings out a clean sweetness instead of a burnt crust, and the night batches crackle in full view of the street.

From bean to cup

The craft journey

  1. 01Green bean

    Sun-dried Liberica from the Johor lowlands and highland lots from Ranau arrive green and dense.

  2. 02Dry roast

    We roast slowly in a dry drum (no margarine, no sugar, no oil) so the bean's own sugars caramelise.

  3. 03Grind

    Steel burrs crack the big Liberica beans to an even size, releasing jackfruit and dark cacao aromas.

  4. 04Brew

    Hot water draws through the grounds by lever, cloth sock or paper cone.

  5. 05Cup

    Served thick and fragrant in a classic kopitiam cup, with a sweet, clean finish.

Brew guides

Starting recipes. Adjust grind to taste.

V60

  • Ratio 1:16 (15 g to 240 ml)
  • Grind Medium-fine
  • Water 92°C
  • Time about 2:45

Bloom with 45 ml for 40 seconds, then pour in slow circles.

AeroPress

  • Ratio 1:13 (18 g to 230 ml)
  • Grind Medium
  • Water 88°C
  • Time about 2:00

Stir five times, steep 90 seconds, then press slowly.

French press

  • Ratio 1:15 (20 g to 300 ml)
  • Grind Coarse
  • Water 94°C
  • Time 4:00

Break the crust at 4 minutes, skim the foam, then plunge gently.
